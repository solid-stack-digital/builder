# Health and startup errors

Main dev/prod and long-running dependency representatives need usable health. `HEALTHCHECK NONE` disables inherited health. A prerequisite must exit 0; a failed or hung job blocks its consumer. A dependency/prerequisite ordering cycle or mixed classification of one target fails before runtime. Requirement-only cycles are allowed, but global E2E still waits for every reachable long-running representative and completed prerequisite. Inspect pending node logs and Docker health status; do not assume all secondary endpoint owners are separate logical health gates. See [lifecycle](../concepts/lifecycle-and-readiness.md).
